## Literature search on rotation and term limits (CLAUDE.md Section 13)
Searched 2026-10-10 (web search; terms: evolutionary game theory, corruption, term limits,
rotation, turnover of enforcers, umpire, replicator). Result: no evolutionary-game model of
corruption with rotation, term limits or turnover of enforcers was found. Closest work:
- Lee et al. 2015 (JTB): enforcer "education" raising the trial rate of honest conduct.
- Zhou et al. 2023 (AMC): higher enforcer exploration rates reduce corruption.
- Liu, Chen, Szolnoki 2019 (M3AS): corrupt enforcers in pool punishment.
- Shi et al. 2023 (AMC): players supervise umpires (extends Lee et al. 2019).
- Abbink 2004 and Buhren 2020: laboratory experiments on staff rotation.
- Ferraz and Finan 2011; Laurent 2021: empirical work on term limits and corruption.
The paper therefore claims novelty only "to our knowledge", and states that fresh-cohort rotation
is formally equivalent to umpire exploration biased toward honesty in the mean field.
